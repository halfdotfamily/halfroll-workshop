# Crown Garden authored environment

Original runtime-authored stonework and botanical geometry lives in `world/crown.js`. No downloaded models or external textures are required by this module.

Two broken arches are assembled from individually beveled stone voussoirs and fluted, coursed columns. Foundations use slope-sampled retaining masonry and inset terrace courses. Mosaic stones, creeping ivy, ground-grown iris beds, wildflower pockets and broken columns frame the central arena while preserving open views. A shallow reflecting basin has a physical bottom, enclosing wall, rim, water and subtle concentric ripples.

The second composition pass replaces isolated tall flowerpots with terrain-following stone-edged gardens. A fragmented mosaic promenade crosses the southern garden; irregular low evergreen borders, layered ferns and clusters of larger cream/iris flowers connect the ruins. The arena itself stays open. Leaf clusters are built from individual leaves and visible stems rather than opaque spheres or hedge boxes.

Two original ghost keeper lanterns are a restrained Aave cultural reference. They are not official Aave logos or a partnership claim. All other architecture and plants are original decorative geometry; none introduces gameplay effects.

Placement respects the actual terrain sampler, the numbered road clearance (1.65 units beyond each prop footprint), the central 9.1-unit arena reserve and the five-unit throne reserve. Details are deterministically placed and instanced by geometry/material. Run `node tools/crown-world/check.mjs` for the authored layout and geometry checks. Browser visual verification is part of the integrated world preview review.
